Hello and welcome! My name is Claire Allen. I am a writer, researcher, and generally curious cat attempting to traverse those nebulous boundaries between intra- and extra-institutional practice. My current interests include social media subcultures and identities, Silicon Valley circlejerks, and the subversion of surveillance technologies.

I am an incoming graduate student at the University of Toronto's Cinema Studies Institute, wherein I hope to further my work on contemporary girlblogging subcultures and their contention with the male gaze. That, and other scholarly projects of mine, can be found here. Other writings of mine can be found here.

Outside of these more serious endeavours I also find myself a collector of visual detritus, especially in digital spaces. This collection can be found on my Instagram (ironic, I know) at @cllathedrals.
